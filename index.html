Helioplan

Plan your solar system before you buy

Sizes panels, battery, charge controller, inverter, cables and fuses for your appliances and your location, then checks that it all works together. For a home, a van or a boat, off-grid or as backup during power outages.

Catches costly mistakes

Frost pushing panel voltage over the controller limit, an inverter that can't start a pump, a battery that can't deliver the current. Each warning shows the value, the limit and what to change. Safety warnings are always free.

Your sun, your climate

Sun and temperature for your exact location from PVGIS and NASA POWER, with a monthly balance that shows the weakest month at once.

Real equipment

More than 1,800 panels, batteries, controllers, standard and hybrid inverters, with specifications from manufacturers' datasheets.

Cables and protection

Cable sizes, fuses and breakers to your country's rules (IEC, NEC, CSA, AS/NZS), a step-by-step installation guide and a PDF report for your electrician.

Helioplan prepares a plan for the system but does not replace an electrical installation design.
